CUBIXIA 1.1.1 LOGIN HOTFIX

This hotfix adds Gmail SMTP/send timeouts so login cannot hang forever before 2-step verification.

Upload the CONTENTS of this folder to GitHub, not just the zip.

After Render redeploys, open:
https://cubixia-1.onrender.com/health
https://cubixia-1.onrender.com/health/email

/health must show version: 1.1.1.
/health/email should answer within 15 seconds. If Gmail is wrong, it will show the real Gmail error instead of hanging.

Render environment variables:
GMAIL_USER = gglitch145@gmail.com
GMAIL_APP_PASSWORD = your Google App Password only

Do not put the app password in GitHub, code, screenshots, or chat.
